# Vending machine pickup candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 200 characters: a pickup line a vending
machine would use on a passing human.

1. Exact change: "Hey. I've been standing here all night with
   your exact change and no expectations." -- smooth, but the
   machine could use its one true asset: it has watched the
   human's routine for months.
2. B4: "You check my prices every day at six. One day you'll
   press B4, and I'll be ready: cold, exact change, no
   judgement." -- the long game, played by an appliance with
   infinite patience.
3. Glow: "Is it the fluorescent light, or did the corridor
   just get brighter? Asking for a machine that is the
   light." -- cute, convoluted.
4. Sold out: "Everything in row three is sold out except my
   feelings for you, which restock nightly." -- cheesy in a
   good way.
5. Coin return: "You pressed coin return on everyone else.
   I kept the light on." -- wistful, less pickup than poem.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. Candidate 1 is a machine being smooth;
candidate 2 is a machine in love -- it has memorised the
human's routine (six o'clock, the price check, the walking
away) and built its whole pitch out of patience: cold, exact
change, no judgement. The long game is the only game a
vending machine can play, and it plays it perfectly.
